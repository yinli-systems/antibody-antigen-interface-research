## Decision

The project will test one falsifiable question:

> Can a model recover cognate antibody--antigen residue-pair geometry and rank
> the correct partner under dual-novel and chronological generalization,
> rather than learning separable paratope and epitope propensities?

The next scientific run is not authorized yet. The current direct split has
zero audited cross-boundary direct-80/80 violations, but it fails the locked
training-retention and quarantine-size gates. The exact joint outer/inner split,
an independent audit, fold-local cache equality, and license receipts must pass
before contact training, model selection, outer-fold scoring, or test inference.

No result, target, or literature value in this protocol is a guarantee of
state-of-the-art or world-leading performance.

### Prospective engineering implementation

The following implementation is an **untrained engineering candidate**, not a
biological result:

- `geometry_labels.py` distinguishes observed-positive contact evidence from
  negative/exact-distance validity. A negative or distance-bin label requires
  explicit heavy-atom-completeness evidence on both residues. It binds Angstrom units,
  altloc, occupancy, assembly, parser, builder-source, input, and output
  provenance; exposes the inclusive `4.5 Angstrom` primary label and `5/6/8 Angstrom`
  sensitivities; and encodes a masked five-DoF, 24-bin periodic relative pose.
- `pair_geometry_schema.py` is the single hash-validated contract shared by
  labels, predictions, and objectives. The model cannot silently use a
  different distance/contact/orientation discretization.
- `pair_geometry.py` implements early bidirectional residue fusion, explicit
  candidate-masked pair states, contact probabilities coupled to distance-bin
  mass, five-axis orientation logits, pair-derived marginals, and a
  double-centered partner score. Decoder heads can be disabled for controlled
  ablations; active pair heads remain quadratic in output size.
- `pair_geometry_losses.py` uses equal-per-complex masked reductions for
  contact, distance, orientation, and Brier terms; derives residue support from
  the candidate mask; rejects contact/distance contradictions; and uses an
  explicit matched-decoy hinge loss rather than treating unknown batch pairs as
  measured negatives.
- `pairformer_evaluator.py` requires a content-addressed frozen residue domain
  and its complete Cartesian pair mask. It reports macro per-complex full-matrix
  AUPRC, conditional rankings, pair-derived marginals, calibration,
  tie-invariant top-k/risk-coverage summaries, true pairwise partner win rate,
  and a 10,000-replicate paired cluster bootstrap that fails closed below ten
  independent clusters.
- `data_admission.py` validates metadata-only source admission. It requires
  externally frozen policy and source-registry digests, five-stage
  raw-to-admitted lineage, per-axis exact/near-duplicate audits, safe canonical
  JSON, license-routing propagation, and separate measured, predicted, and
  synthetic counts. It does not inspect or prove the referenced dataset bytes,
  scientific truth, legal interpretation, or auditor identity.
- `hard_negative_contract.py` v2 keeps unknown pairings out of binary
  non-binder supervision. Measured non-binders and mutation flips require exact
  externally anchored pair/assay evidence; all controls bind the nested
  train-only role, residue domain, candidate policy, patch or pose definition,
  and source lineage.
- `gate0_authority.py` requires an externally expected complete receipt digest
  for every one of the six Gate 0 domains. Producer/auditor identifiers must be
  distinct where an independent audit is required, but those identifiers are
  provenance fields rather than signatures. Even a passed Gate 0 decision can
  grant only contact training and inner model selection; it cannot grant outer
  or external-test evaluation.
- `pairformer_training_step.py` is a train-only mathematical reference for
  equal-per-complex geometry optimization. It binds exact batch, label,
  hard-negative, Gate 0, source, model, seed, collective, optimizer, scaler,
  RNG, and checkpoint receipts. Its SUM packet and fixed-world-size DDP
  backward scalar reject rank-local-mean averaging. It retains all
  microbatch graphs until the reduction boundary and simulates rather than
  launches collectives, so it is not the production high-scale hot path.
- The finite-cache runtime uses unique valid samples and neutral padded tail
  slots, exact cursor/checkpoint restoration, fixed-world-size DDP semantics,
  and hash-before-deserialization cache verification. Its sampler is capped at
  `16,777,216` int64 indices (`128 MiB` per rank) and deliberately rejects
  billion-scale use. Such use needs a separately audited stateless/block
  shuffle or sharded index.
- The engineering-cache v2 builder requires externally anchored train-only
  authority, canonical-source, builder, and per-side embedding-store manifests;
  exact allowlist equality; no-follow descriptor reads; hash-before-load; and
  create-only sealed publication. It is an in-memory engineering cache, not a
  scientific or billion-scale training pipeline.
- The v5 nested workflow is a non-authorizing exact core-feasibility precursor.
  It accepts only the physical train-only allowlist and its exact four-column
  train-only canonical manifest; the full authority split/test manifest must
  not be mounted or read. No-follow, same-descriptor reads and an isolated
  Python process stage the exact three-source application closure. A
  create-only `provisional_discovery` attempt publishes only a candidate
  membership digest and append-only attempt ledger. An external controller
  must freeze that digest before a separate, numbered
  `externally_pinned_replay`, and an independent replay audit may accept only
  that pinned replay. Every v2, v3, and v4 artifact or receipt is
  non-authoritative. v5 adds exact component-presence variables and minimums
  of 25 components per axis in each outer validation fold and 20 per axis in
  each inner validation fold. Component identity is the lexical representative
  of a transitive connected component reconstructed from the complete
  authority-train direct graph, and that identity is retained when an inner
  universe omits a bridge entity. The builder binds both component roles into
  every selected-vector ledger; the auditor independently reconstructs the
  complete graphs and rejects substituted, unknown, axis-swapped, or
  heldout-induced component identities. This is a diversity constraint, not a
  claim of global S2 or transitive lineage independence. Even an audited v5
  replay proves only feasibility of its published exact
  core model: it cannot close split-domain Gate 0 or authorize training,
  selection, or evaluation. The locked full-retention lexicographic stages,
  representativeness and remaining balance gates, deterministic sequential
  fixing and canonical tie-break trace, precision/power receipt, remaining
  runtime closure, organizational independence, a second independent MILP,
  and all other Gate 0 domains remain outstanding. Direct neighborhoods do not
  prove transitive lineage independence, and replay auditing does not
  independently prove the solver's historical execution. Full-graph
  reconstruction and solver workspace also have unmeasured production cost.
- The distributed launch contract binds source, runtime, topology, execution
  mode, and externally verified runtime evidence. It rejects an unreviewed
  96-GPU job and does not implement FSDP2. The associated scale runbook is a
  prospective authorization plan, not evidence that an 8-, 32-, or 64-GPU
  PAIRFormer model was trained.

## Test firewall and freeze order

1. Hash and freeze raw sources, normalized entities, chain mappings, contact
   code, split inputs, and software/runtime manifests.
2. Solve the three outer plus nine dependent inner assignments jointly, or use
   an exact decomposition whose equivalence is documented. A sequential
   single-level solve must not be described as a joint solution.
3. Independently audit all declared antibody and antigen sequence views,
   structural neighbors, group distributions, retention, quarantine, and cache
   membership. Approximate search may propose candidates but cannot certify
   zero leakage.
4. Materialize fold-local caches. The model process may read only the active
   training allowlist; it may not mount the whole pool or parse non-training
   sequences, labels, structures, contacts, or group statistics.
5. Use inner validation only for architecture, loss, hard-negative policy,
   calibration, threshold, epoch, and checkpoint selection.
6. Freeze source tree, environment, seeds, candidate, checkpoint rule, score
   aggregation, threshold, calibration, and one-shot registry before outer
   inference.
7. Score each outer fold once for the preregistered analysis. No outer result
   may change the candidate family or hyperparameters.
8. Freeze the final promoted system before a one-shot external or chronological
   test. A fresh prospective set remains sealed until that freeze.

Any test exposure, failed attempt, amendment, or schema inspection must be
preserved in an append-only ledger. The historical MIPE supplied test split is
an external test-split evaluation with pre-scoring schema access, never a
pristine blind test.

## Model and negative-control ladder

The minimum comparison is:

- **D0:** locked dual encoder and bidirectional cognate-partner retrieval.
- **R1:** early bidirectional residue fusion with partner ranking.
- **R2:** R1 plus an explicit antibody-residue x antigen-residue contact head.
- **PAIRFormer-S:** contact, distance, orientation, interaction-type,
  confidence, and uncertainty heads; marginal epitope/paratope scores are
  derived or secondary.
- **PAIRFormer scale ladder:** approximately 100M, 300M, 650M, and 1.3B only
  after Gate 1; no 3B run without a positive scaling result.

The antigen side may combine sequence, intrachain residue graph, solvent
surface, curvature/normals, physicochemistry, and structure uncertainty.
Bound-complex coordinates or interchain distances are forbidden inputs for a
deployable monomer-input track and may appear only in a labeled oracle track.

Train-only hard negatives must include matched wrong partners, mutation-induced
binding flips, wrong epitope patches, near-native incorrect docking poses,
partner shuffles, unary/null inputs, and structure/surface feature shuffles.
Unobserved SAbDab pairings are not measured non-binders and must not be labeled
as such.

## Metrics and statistical contract

The primary pair endpoint is per-complex macro full-matrix AUPRC over the frozen
candidate domain. The primary contact definition is any non-hydrogen atom pair
within `4.5 Angstrom`; `5/6/8 Angstrom` are sensitivity analyses and cannot replace the
primary result.

Required endpoints are:

- pair macro AUPRC, AUROC, MCC, F1/Dice, Precision@K, Recall@K, and conditional
  row/column ranking;
- epitope and paratope macro AUPRC/MCC/F1 as secondary endpoints;
- bidirectional cognate retrieval Recall@1/5/10, MRR, normalized rank, and
  pairwise win rate;
- NLL, Brier score, adaptive ECE, and risk--coverage after validation-only
  calibration;
- 10,000 paired cluster-level bootstrap replicates;
- at least three fixed seeds for screening and five fixed seeds for a promoted
  final comparison;
- correction for the finite preregistered family of primary tests.

Pooled micro metrics are secondary. Evaluation always uses the complete frozen
candidate matrix; negative subsampling is a training method, not an evaluation
shortcut. The strongest separable unary/additive/outer-product marginal
baseline, degree-aware random baseline, raw-embedding baseline, and matched
partner controls must be reported.

## Sequential gates

### Gate 0 -- integrity, legality, and split authority

Pass only when license receipts, exact joint nested splits, independent
leakage audits, retention/quarantine/distribution gates, fold-cache equality,
runtime locks, and immutable manifests all pass. Until then, only train-only
engineering diagnostics are allowed.

### Gate 1 -- genuine partner dependence

Pass only when, on strict development splits:

- the 95% paired cluster-bootstrap lower bound for pair macro-AUPRC improvement
  over the strongest separable marginal baseline is above zero;
- matched partner shuffle causes a significant deterioration;
- bidirectional cognate retrieval exceeds degree-aware permutation and D0;
- antibody-only, antigen-only, null, composition, and similarity controls do
  not explain the gain;
- the direction holds across the fixed seed set and major antigen strata.

If Gate 1 fails, improve labels, candidate masks, partner supervision, and hard
negatives. Do not increase model scale.

### Gate 2 -- useful scaling

Train the 100M/300M/650M/1.3B ladder only after Gate 1. Continue beyond 650M
only if the harder split endpoints improve by approximately one absolute AUPRC
point or more with stable uncertainty, and the systems audit shows acceptable
throughput per GPU-hour. A larger parameter count is not itself a result.

### Gate 3 -- docking utility

Freeze pair-to-restraint conversion on validation. On an identical
chronological docking benchmark, require paired improvement in DockQ success
and interface RMSD, calibrated restraints to beat binary top-k restraints, and
no material increase in high-confidence false restraints. Pair AUPRC without
docking utility does not pass this gate.

### Gate 4 -- prospective experimental confirmation

Only after computational freeze and institutional approval, test at least three
unseen antigens with blind candidate selection and prespecified SPR/BLI,
expression, SEC, aggregation, stability, and epitope/escape validation. Predicted
structure scores are filters, not experimental binding evidence.

## Compute contract

The audited partition exposes 12 nodes with eight RTX 5090 GPUs per node:
96 configured GPUs with `32,607 MiB` each. The current QoS caps a single job at
eight nodes, so the operational single-job maximum is **64 GPUs**, not 96.
The nodes have no NVLink; cross-node traffic uses dual 25-Gb links, and audited
64-GPU jobs `1313384--1313389` completed correct all-reduce checks. The measured
64 MiB median was `38.44 ms` (`3.4367 GB/s` bus bandwidth); the 512 MiB median
was `861.49 ms`, with `1,309.65 ms` p95 and `1.2269 GB/s` bus bandwidth. These
are topology diagnostics, not model-scaling results, and show material
large-message jitter.

Therefore:

- use independent fold/seed/data-generation/hard-negative jobs as the default
  scale-out axis;
- prefer measured 2--4 GPU jobs for the current small D0 path;
- use 8-GPU pods for larger models only after per-node profiling;
- use up to 64 synchronous GPUs only after all-reduce, all-gather,
  reduce-scatter, dataloader, checkpoint, and failure-recovery gates pass;
- do not claim a 96-GPU synchronous run without a new scheduler grant and a
  verified cross-job rendezvous design.

Required systems reporting includes actual world size, node count, model and
trainable parameters, admitted residues/tokens, GPU-hours, useful
residues/second, scaling efficiency, peak memory, checkpoint/recovery time,
failed-job rate, runtime/container hashes, and communication percentiles.

## Identical-benchmark claim boundary

"Better," "best," "state of the art," and "world-leading" are forbidden unless
the compared results use the same:

- raw dataset revision and admitted complex IDs;
- train/validation/test assignments and permitted training data;
- antibody/antigen novelty and chronology rules;
- input regime and structure provenance;
- residue candidate domain, resolved mask, and contact cutoff;
- metric implementation, aggregation unit, seed policy, checkpoint selection,
  calibration, and uncertainty procedure.

Author-reported results on a different split are contextual references only.
An improvement over a local baseline may be claimed only within the exact
local benchmark named in the result artifact.
