# External research collaboration brief v1

This is a drafting asset only. No message has been sent.

## One-paragraph description

We are developing a sequence-input antibody--antigen residue-pair contact model
with predicted intra-chain geometry, explicit partner-swap controls, and an
append-only evidence protocol. The central question is whether partner-specific
contacts generalize after simultaneous antibody- and antigen-side similarity
control. Current work scales pair-contact supervision on PINDER-Train and
builds a four-view VH/VL/concatenated-antibody/antigen leakage audit; previously
opened public cohorts are retained as analyst-exposed evidence rather than
relabeled blind tests.

## Concrete collaboration asks

1. Independent execution of a frozen post-cutoff or sealed-label cohort.
2. Review of four-view sequence-cold split rules and discarded bridge systems.
3. Access to non-public binding measurements for prospective validation, with
   labels retained by the data holder until predictions are frozen.
4. Experimental triage of a small, preregistered contact-guided CDR proposal
   set after surrogate and developability filters pass.

## Evidence to attach

- frozen protocol and source SHA-256 receipts;
- exact cohort membership hash, without restricted raw data;
- predeclared tasks, seeds, metrics, and zero-positive policy;
- evidence classification: train-only, development, analyst-exposed, or sealed;
- negative canary decisions as well as successful gates.

## Do not claim

Do not claim affinity, successful antibody design, AlphaFold/RFantibody
equivalence, or blind external generalization before the corresponding sealed
or experimental evidence exists.
