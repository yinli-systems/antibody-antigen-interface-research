Original main README uses the September 30 operations snapshot: 12/36 PLM tasks, structure RePaRank incomplete. Final receipts record 36/36 with no promotion and centered RePaRank 217/217. Correct the generator, not only its output. Keep scheduler snapshots historical, not falsely live.

The preserved archive has 17 snapshots and 747 evidence-path links. This audit has 30 HEADs,103,552 repeated tree entries,4,913 distinct path/blob versions and 4,756 Git blobs. Units and observation dates must remain separate.

## 3. Completed evidence is scattered across side branches

Final rescue, centered RePaRank and structural-PLM evidence is not all stored locally on main. Fixed-commit links resolve this without merging experiment implementations or moving hash-bound files.

## 4. The October 4 manuscript has an evidence-publication gap

Latest main adds reviewer Markdown reporting 75 ablation models,75 improvement models, new OpenDDE, specialist and AsEP experiments. Matching terminal artifacts were not identified in the 30 HEAD trees. The earlier 36-task running ledger and synthetic integration test cannot verify those later results. Mark them report-only, not false or never run.

To close the gap, attach condition/seed/run membership, source/split hashes, selection freezes, terminal receipts, per-seed/per-complex metrics, uncertainty outputs and raw-prediction location/hashes. Existing off-Git artifacts may suffice; this audit does not require rerunning training.

## 5. Counts and analysis authority need reconciliation

The report’s 75 further models are described as rounds of 6 and 12 arms×3 seeds: that accounts for 54, not 75 without extra entries. The earlier frozen plan uses crossed seed×complex uncertainty; the newer report says simultaneous PDB-block bootstrap after seed averaging. Link the actual revised analysis authority and freeze, rather than silently conflating protocols.

## 6. EPIC v2 needs model identity and selection-transfer context

Original submitted checkpoint is not exactly restored. Reconstructed AF3 scores cannot be labelled as the original submission. The contact-selected ensemble fails to beat the unchanged-model ensemble on test, while the paratope-selected ensemble happens to lead all tasks. Separate successful paratope selection, exploratory cross-task observations and seed-ensemble versus component contributions. Check ambiguous as-published labels.

## 7. Identical sample counts cause false comparisons

Development 217 is not holdout 217; historical test 549 is not later development 549. A checkpoint-specific invalid 455 cache does not invalidate official 455 under another authority. Use the cohort guide before creating any ranking.

## 8. AP was incorrectly translated into top-k precision

The decoder README infers 97.6% false top-ranked pairs from AP≈0.024. That is not a valid interpretation of AP. Its decoder-choice-does-not-matter heading also exceeds what one seed establishes. Keep the original file but place this correction at the entry route and report actual P@k/recall metrics.

## 9. Pair structure is not proven partner identification

Exceeding the model’s marginal outer product establishes a within-complex scoring margin. It does not alone show true-antigen identification among alternatives. The full-grid counterevidence and newer near-chance decoy report must be presented alongside it. Head/contact consistency is also not partner specificity.

## 10. Estimator and uncertainty language remains important

The claims ledger correctly distinguishes ratio-of-means and mean-of-ratios near its top, but still says mean of per-seed ratios throughout later. Do not unify these numbers by renaming. Keep component/complex weighting, seed/block uncertainty and pointwise/simultaneous intervals separate. A zero-crossing interval is not equivalence, safety or universal failure.

## 11. Newer prose has scope and coverage inconsistencies

OpenDDE full-completion wording must retain the disclosed long-entity AF3 fallback. AsEP external-diagnostic versus no-external-test wording needs consistent chronology/scope. Reported label matches are 3,047/3,208, or 3,169 at tolerance: do not introduce 3,208/3,208 float 16 without its actual certificate. AUTHOR TO CONFIRM fields remain unresolved.

## 12. Negative results and application boundaries are part of the work

Retain structural-PLM no-promotion, clean-input rescue damage, cheap-gate controls, failed OOD admission, non-replicated MRR anomaly, Hadamard non-win and AbCite LoRA regression. AbCite retrieval is a separate application, not more antibody-prediction evidence. Branch copies are not replications.
