# EPIC camera-ready claim boundaries

1. **Accepted table versus post-submission evidence.** The submitted OpenDDE Table 1 remains historical. AF3-based retraining, later FrameContact experiments, and new post-processing analyses are labelled post-submission.
2. **No AsEP backfill.** The abstract's exact-EPIC AsEP claim is removed rather than satisfied using a different successor architecture.
3. **No predictor-equivalence claim.** AF3 and bound-geometry sensitivity do not prove bitwise or checkpoint-matched equivalence to OpenDDE.
4. **No SOTA leaderboard claim.** Imported literature values are not treated as same-protocol comparisons.
5. **No prospective-OOD claim.** The official 455 test is historical/exposed; later antigen-cluster and AsEP analyses are separate evidence classes.
6. **No causal component claim from single-seed screens.** Geometry/count-loss/decoder screens are identified by seed count and authority. Claims are moderated when intervals are unresolved.
7. **No favorable metric substitution.** Contact AP/AUPRC and prevalence are primary; AUROC and recall are secondary. Pooled and macro metrics are named separately.
8. **No undefined expert.** TOPK2 is removed from the EPIC manuscript unless fully defined and evaluated as a separate post-submission model.
